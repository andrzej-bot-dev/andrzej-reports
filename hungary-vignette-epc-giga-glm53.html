1 · Executive summary

The letter is authentic and its mandate is real. Euro Parking Collection plc is officially listed by Magyar Közút as its foreign collection intermediary (with Contractum Ltd), alongside UAI Solutions GmbH (DACH), Petkov & Co (SK/CZ) and Solventis/UAI Inkasso (RO).

Amounts verified exactly against the official 2026 tariff: D1/D1M/D2/U surcharge = 27 790 HUF (60 days) / 95 730 HUF (after). Admin costs (19 012,67 HUF / 575,58 kr) are the collector's margin permitted by Decree 45/2020 (XI.28) ITM §11(7).

Legal character: the "lag nr 1988:1 om vägtransporter (Kkt.)" in the letter is a Swedish rendering of Hungary's Act I of 1988 on road transport (1988. évi I. törvény), §33/A — not Swedish law. The pótdíj is a public-law charge enforced through civil procedure (payment order → lawsuit → enforcement).

Enforcement into Sweden on paper: Kronofogden requires a foreign judgment/decision + international agreement (sometimes exequatur). Brussels Ia excludes revenue claims; FD 2005/214 covers criminal penalties only; EPC is UK-based post-Brexit. In practice: no documented case ever of a Hungarian vignette surcharge enforced against a Swedish/Nordic resident — the pattern is 2–4 escalating letters, sometimes a second DCA (Trace), then silence.

2 · EPC plc & Verra Mobility

Who: UK company (PO Box 897, Hayes; complaints PO Box 149, Dartford). European arm of Verra Mobility Corp (NASDAQ: VRRM, Mesa AZ) — toll & violation management in 15+ European countries; also collects UK tolls/ULEZ for foreign vehicles and Norwegian tolls.

Model: issuing authority hands over foreign-plate cases → EPC pulls keeper data from national registries (DVLA, RDW; SE analogue) → localized letters (site in sv/de/hu/es/pl; Swedish bankgiro account) → collects + admin margin.

Legitimacy: ADAC on sister firm UAI: "ordnungsgemäß beauftragt... Ein Betrug liegt hier nicht vor." Hungarian official page confirms EPC's appointment.

Criticism history: EP Written Question E-2010-4132 (inflating fines ~150% in Brussels); Brussels Times 2024 (unlawful Belgian data route via Antwerp bailiff); persistent forum reports of fines despite purchased vignettes (plate typos, category mismatches); Verra itself faces 2026 US securities class actions (investor matters, unrelated).

Verdict: legitimate collector, real debt under Hungarian law — but aggressive formula letters and a documented history of data-law shortcuts and error-driven claims.

  5. One fine per plate per calendar day (also across midnight if ≤60 min).
  6. Do nothing — see risks.
